## Frontend Slides Fixed-Stage Policy

When this design system is used by the `frontend-slides` skill, generate the final deck as a **fixed 1920×1080 stage** scaled uniformly to the viewport. Preserve the 16:9 canvas on every screen; letterbox or pillarbox, never reflow. All pixel values in this file are already in 1920×1080 stage coordinates.

If the team also needs a **phone-portrait (1080×1920) version**, keep the same tokens and components on a 1080×1920 stage: the dot-matrix moves above the content, the audience row stays at the bottom, and columns stack. Do not shrink type below the sizes in the table below.

A working reference deck lives next to this file: `example.html`. Read it only if a detail below is ambiguous; never copy its demo content into a user's deck.

## Overview

Seed Academy is the Seed Academy (习得学堂) team's own presentation look, distilled from the school-assembly decks our audience already trusts (parent meetings, class briefings, programme launches). The premise is **one voice in a crowd**: slides are populated with calm fields of identical green dots, and exactly one of them is an orange speech bubble. That single dot does all the storytelling work — it marks the speaker on the cover, the current idea in a list, and the current page in the progress rail.

The system has two surfaces and they alternate:

- **Forest stage** (`{colors.forest}`, optionally a very subtle top-to-bottom deepening to `{colors.forest-deep}` — the only gradient allowed) for the cover, chapter openers and keyword-list slides. Type is near-white `{colors.white}`.
- **Classroom board** (`{colors.board}`) for reading slides — statements, explanations, quotes. Type is charcoal `{colors.ink}`, emphasis in signal red.

Typography is deliberately plain and heavy: **Noto Sans SC at 900** for every headline and keyword, **700** for reading paragraphs, **500** only for small supporting copy. Latin numerals and dates use **Outfit** so `2026.10.10` looks engineered, not default. There is no serif anywhere, no italic, no condensed face.

**Density philosophy: assembly-hall legible.** These decks are projected in classrooms and halls and read from the back row. One title + up to five keywords, or one title + one paragraph of at most ~70 Chinese characters, is a full slide. If content is longer, split it.

**Key Characteristics**
- Deep forest-green stage alternating with a pale whiteboard surface.
- Fields of soft green round dots; exactly one terracotta speech-dot per slide.
- Heavy Chinese sans at 900 for titles and keyword lists; body at 700 with generous 2.0+ line-height.
- Key phrases in signal red (light slides) / orange (dark slides) — colour only, never boxes or underlines.
- Cover footer of round heads and arch bodies (an abstract audience).
- Bottom progress rail of dots on reading slides, with the speech-dot on the current page.

Rules:
- **Orange is a single-object colour.** One speech-dot per slide (on board slides it lives in the progress rail), plus emphasised words on dark slides. Never an orange background, never orange headlines.
- **Red is text-only** and only on light slides.
- Green dots on dark slides use `leaf`; on light slides the dots are `ink-dot`. Never put green dots on the light board.
- No other accent colours. Charts, if unavoidable, use `leaf` / `forest-mid` / `orange` only.

## Layout Archetypes

Build every deck from these five. Each is defined at 1920×1080.

1. **Cover (forest)** — Logo centred near the top of the right two-thirds; title + spaced subtitle + Outfit date centred in that column. Left third: a 9-column × 9-row dot-matrix of small (14px) `leaf` dots with one speech-dot. Bottom 360px: the audience row (13 heads ~130px diameter, arches below, a hairline of 8px dots above).
2. **Keyword list (forest)** — Title top-left at 112px. Left column (x 160–480): a 5-column dot-matrix running the full height with one speech-dot (optionally on the row level of the item being discussed). The item being discussed is full white; the others drop to `white-2`. Right: 3–5 keyword rows at 72px with white dot-bullets.
3. **Statement / reading (board)** — Title-marker + 76px title top-left. One paragraph at 52px/2.1 with red emphasis, left-aligned, max-width ~1400px. Progress rail at bottom (y ≈ 990), logo bottom-right.
4. **Two-column compare / cards (board or forest)** — Title as in 3. Two or three equal columns separated by 1px `border-light` / `border-dark` rules; each column: a 22px label, then items as round dot + 900-weight 46px line. On board slides the progress rail already holds the slide's speech-dot, so column bullets stay plain dots. No rounded cards, no shadows.
5. **Media (forest)** — Title top-left; image or video in a `forest-deep` well with 1px `border-dark`, square corners. A small dot-matrix may sit in the empty corner, with the speech-dot pointing toward the media.

Spacing: pad 96px / 130px; title-to-content gap ≥ 72px; keep the bottom 120px of board slides free for the progress rail.

## Motion

- Content: fade-up 28px, `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` 0.7s, 80–120ms stagger.
- Dot fields: dots fade in with an 18ms-per-dot ripple from the top-left; the speech-dot arrives last with a springy pop (`cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` 0.6s).
- Progress rail: the speech-dot glides to the current index (transform, 0.5s) on slide change.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do**
- Put exactly one orange speech-dot on every slide and make it mean something (speaker, current item, current page).
- Alternate forest and board surfaces; never three of the same surface in a row.
- Keep titles to ≤ 8 CJK characters where possible; keyword lists to 2–6 characters per item.
- Use red for the 2–4 phrases that carry the paragraph's argument.

**Don't**
- Don't use serif or italic faces, or Inter/Roboto/Arial as display.
- Don't add drop shadows, glassmorphism, rounded cards, or multi-colour gradients.
- Don't place more than one speech-dot, or make the speech-dot a different colour.
- Don't put green dots on the light board or red text on the forest stage.
- Don't crowd: if a paragraph exceeds ~70 CJK characters, split the slide.

## Iteration Guide

1. New slide? Pick one of the five archetypes; do not invent a sixth unless the content truly needs it.
2. New emphasis? Colour the phrase (`red` on board, `orange` on forest) — nothing else.
3. New decorative element? It must be made of the dot vocabulary (round dots + one speech-dot).
4. New colour? No.
